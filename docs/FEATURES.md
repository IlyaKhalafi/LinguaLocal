## Reading preferences

Settings offers larger reading text, comfortable line spacing, a bundled Atkinson Hyperlegible font, higher contrast, and a quiet mode that removes decorative art and animations. These choices stay in the current browser. The controls remain collapsed until needed.

![Reading controls](screenshots/reading-settings.png)

### Timed transcripts

Replay a captioned passage by its timestamp. Select text to look it up in the dictionary. Repeat controls stay folded until needed.

![Timed transcript](screenshots/timed-transcript.png)

### Dictation from book audio

Open a timed transcript and choose listening practice. Replay short passages, type what you hear, and check individual words. Checked passages count toward private progress, including when leaving early. Capitalization and punctuation do not affect the score.

![Dictation](screenshots/dictation.png)

[Answer correction](screenshots/dictation-correction.png) · [Mobile](screenshots/dictation-correction-mobile.png) · [Dark appearance](screenshots/dictation-dark.png) · [Result](screenshots/dictation-complete.png)

### Written and listening practice

Choose spelling, local listening, or gap fill. Use the whole dictionary, a level, or your saved words. Keyboard answers receive immediate feedback. Listening uses an English voice installed on the device.

![Practice](screenshots/practice-playing.png)

[Answer correction](screenshots/practice-correction.png) · [Mobile](screenshots/practice-correction-mobile.png)

### Dark appearance

Use light or dark colours, or follow the device setting. Preferences survive a reload, while print views use light colours. Books retain their recognisable covers.

![Dark appearance](screenshots/dark-overview.png)

### Lesson vocabulary review

Check an answer, mark the lesson complete, and review its own words without another setup screen. Continue with the next lesson at the same level. Reviews remain in the current browser.

![Lesson review](screenshots/lesson-review.png)

[Completed lesson](screenshots/lesson-completed.png) · [Revealed meaning](screenshots/lesson-review-flipped.png) · [Review result](screenshots/lesson-review-complete.png)

### Printed classroom materials

The owner command creates vocabulary and lesson worksheets with separate answer keys. Open the generated files locally and print them or save them as PDF. Student pages do not gain authoring controls.

![Vocabulary worksheet](screenshots/worksheet-vocabulary.png)

### Personal notes

Keep a private note beside a word or below a lesson. The editor opens on request and travels with progress backups. Other tabs update saved notes without replacing an unfinished draft.

![Personal word note](screenshots/word-note.png)

[Mobile word note](screenshots/word-note-mobile.png) · [Lesson note](screenshots/lesson-note.png) · [Mobile lesson note](screenshots/lesson-note-mobile.png) · [Dark appearance](screenshots/dark-word-note.png) · [Dark mobile](screenshots/dark-word-note-mobile.png)

### Private study decks

Create a named deck in the review screen and add dictionary words using the small deck control. Tags filter the available words. Decks stay in this browser and travel with progress backups. Simultaneous edits offer reload or deliberate overwrite. Removing a deck keeps notes and review history.

![Study decks](screenshots/study-decks.png)

[Deck controls](screenshots/deck-manager.png) · [Mobile](screenshots/deck-manager-mobile.png) · [Word membership](screenshots/word-decks.png) · [Mobile membership](screenshots/word-decks-mobile.png) · [Concurrent edit](screenshots/deck-conflict.png) · [Mobile edit](screenshots/deck-conflict-mobile.png) · [Review ratings](screenshots/review-ratings.png) · [Mobile ratings](screenshots/review-ratings-mobile.png)

Hard and easy ratings are folded beneath the two regular review buttons. Difficult practice appears after repeated forgotten answers and clears after three successful recalls.
